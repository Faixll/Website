RULES
- Minimum size of the full round logo: 120 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print. Below that use the favicon/small version.
- Keep clear space around it equal to the height of the cleaver.
- Do not stretch, recolour, add shadows or change the text. Put it on any colour; it is already a solid disc.
- The Arabic spelling used is البدع and the English ALBADA. Confirm with the client before printing.
- No trademark / existing-logo check was done.
